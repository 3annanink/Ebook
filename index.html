The 4-stage batching method

Five hours a week. Zero context switching.

Switching between writing, picking images and checking notifications kills momentum. Four single-focus sprints fix that.

90min

Ideation & Research

Audit your last 30 days, mine comments and competitor threads, and capture at least 15 raw premises without filtering.

120min

Drafting & Hooking

Pick 7–10 winners, write the full bodies fast, and create 3 hook variations for every draft.

60min

Editing & Formatting

Cut filler, tighten sentences, format for mobile, and choose the strongest hook for each post.

30min

Scheduling & Staging

Load everything into your queue, set links and tags, and block time for the first engagement window.

90 + 120 + 60 + 30 = 300 minutes, one weekly production cycle.

Chapter 3

Attention only pays when it has somewhere to go.

Threads, X and LinkedIn reward informal directness, but reach without a conversion path is financially worthless. Build a 3-stage organic funnel.

Funnel stageObjectivePrimary contentConversion
Top of FunnelBroad discovery, visibility and algorithmic reachHigh-resonance hooks, relatable takes, provocative opinionsPlatform follow / profile click
Middle of FunnelAuthority building, trust development, qualificationDeep-dive breakdowns, tutorials, systems, curated listsLead magnet opt-in / newsletter
Bottom of FunnelCommercial transaction, client acquisitionTransformation proof, product tours, feature-benefit case studiesDirect checkout / sales call booking

Make your bio a landing page

State who you help, the outcome you deliver, and one trackable link with a clear incentive.

Bridge with a free lead magnet

Never send cold traffic to an expensive offer. Capture emails first with a template, checklist or swipe file.

The P.S. CTA technique

Keep the thread 100% valuable, then place your commercial bridge only in the final post or sign-off.

Organic DM triggers

Invite a reply keyword, then add a short, genuine personal note before sending the resource link.
